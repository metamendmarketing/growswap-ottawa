# Page Specifications

## `/`
Hero: "Ottawa is Growing"
Animated metrics, living map, Harvest Pulse, best swaps, Produce Exchange table, 2027 planning insight, activity feed.

## `/map`
Full-screen living map with filters, timeline, radius slider, layer toggles, list/map split view.

## `/market`
Tabs: Everything, Picked Today, Available Now, Swap, Free, Coming Soon, Surplus Rescue.

## `/market/[id]`
Photography, quantity, harvest age, location distance, price, GrowSwap value, trade preferences, grower card, supply/demand chart, similar listings.

## `/swap`
Best matches, 2-way matches, multi-way matches, graph/map visualization, offer workflow.

## `/garden`
Garden visualizer, beds, current crops, yield forecast, expected household use, projected surplus, potential local value.

## `/plan`
Questionnaire + network-aware 2027 recommendations. Show current plan vs optimized plan.

## `/ottawa`
Food Grid: active growers, available pounds, exchange value, rescued food, supply/demand matrix, heatmaps, top shortages/surpluses.

## `/ottawa/product/[slug]`
Local reference value, 7D/30D/season/1Y chart, supply, demand, growers, peak harvest, upcoming inventory, map.

## `/community/[slug]`
Local metrics, top available, top wanted, upcoming harvest, challenges, active growers.

## `/grower/[username]`
Profile, approximate area, rating, garden images, current harvests, coming soon, wants, reviews.

## `/admin`
Users, listings, products, pricing, reports, map data, demo reset, date controls, seed controls.
