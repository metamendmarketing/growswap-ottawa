# Five-Minute Demo Script

## 1. Ottawa is Growing
Open dashboard. Show 486+ growers, 1,800+ lb available, 6,000+ lb coming soon. Change radius and toggle Available -> Growing -> 2027 Plans.

## 2. Product intelligence
Click a tomato cluster. Open Ottawa Tomato Index. Show current value, price chart, supply, demand, peak harvest, and neighbourhood heatmap.

## 3. My Garden
Open Alex's Garden. Show 6 tomato plants, expected yield, household use, projected surplus, potential trade value.

## 4. SwapMatch
Click Find Swaps. Show 2-way options, then "Better Match Found". Open deterministic 3-way swap: Alex tomatoes -> David, David potatoes -> Sarah, Sarah eggs -> Alex. Animate map routes.

## 5. 2027 Planner
Open Plan. Click Optimize. Reduce zucchini, increase garlic/onions/potatoes. Show local supply/demand explanation.

## 6. Ottawa Food Grid
Finish with citywide supply, demand, rescued food, active gardens, and projected shortages. Closing line: "Instead of 500 separate backyards, GrowSwap treats Ottawa as one interconnected farm."
